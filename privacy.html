Counter Atlas

Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

This policy covers Counter Atlas Uploader, the tool the Counter Atlas channel uses to publish its own videos to YouTube through the YouTube Data API.

Who uses the app

Only the owner of the Counter Atlas YouTube channel. The app is not offered to the public, and no one else signs in to it.

What the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app requests permission to upload videos and to manage the channel's videos, thumbnails and captions. It uses that access only to publish Counter Atlas videos to the Counter Atlas channel.

What the app stores

The sign-in token is stored only on the channel owner's computer. The app keeps a record of the videos it uploaded (video IDs and links). It does not collect, store or share any personal information about viewers or any other users.

Sharing

No data obtained through the YouTube API is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

YouTube and Google

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and data is handled according to the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time from Google account permissions, which also removes it from the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the Counter Atlas channel page.
