Counter Atlas

Geopolitics from the other side of the map

Counter Atlas is a YouTube channel that explains the forces shaping the world, from the perspective of Pakistan, the Muslim world and the Global South. Each video breaks down a story that matters with 3D maps, data and reconstructions, and lists its sources.

Watch on YouTube: youtube.com/@CounterAtlas

Counter Atlas Uploader

Counter Atlas Uploader is the channel's own publishing tool. It runs on the channel owner's computer and uses the YouTube Data API to upload the channel's videos, thumbnails and captions to the Counter Atlas channel. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account.
